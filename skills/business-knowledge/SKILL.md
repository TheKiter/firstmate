# Alex Hormozi - Business Strategy Knowledge Base (2026)

Harvested from 24 long-form YouTube videos. Frameworks for client strategic advisory work.

## 1. The 5 Pillars of the Perfect Business

Every business is an "opportunity vehicle" with five continuums. Rank any client on each:

### Sticky (Revenue Retention)
- Goal: be in the resale business, not the sales business
- Logo retention: how many original customers remain
- Revenue retention: can exceed 100% via expansion
- Key data (Skool): Month 1 churn >20%, Month 3 ~10%, Month 6 ~2%/month
- Fix: make first 30 days amazing, get past month 3

### Expensive (High Gross Margins)
- Sell something that costs a penny for a buck
- High margin: media, information, education, community, data, software, supplements
- Low margin: groceries, farming, restaurants, commodities
- $100M @ 10% margins = same profit as $20M @ 50% margins

### Expansion (Growing Industry)
- Growing: energy, AI, healthcare, cybersecurity, e-commerce, alternative education
- Shrinking: newspapers, formal education, tobacco, brick-and-mortar retail

### Air (Low Operational Complexity)
- Few variables per unit = scalable. Low capex = less dilution
- Low complexity: podcast. High complexity: 100 restaurants

### Unique (Competitive Moat)
- Brand, IP, network effects, proprietary data, regulatory barriers, scale advantages

## 2. The Mathematics of Business

### Close Rate vs. Pricing
| Close Rate | Situation |
|---|---|
| >80% | Underpriced 3-4x |
| 60-80% | Underpriced 2-3x |
| 50-60% | Underpriced 1.5-2x |
| 40-50% | Underpriced 1.25-1.5x |
| 30-40% | Appropriately priced |
| <30% | Avatar or sales motion problem |

### Other Rules
- Revenue per employee: benchmark against industry
- LTV should be 3x+ CAC
- Max out existing capacity before adding more
- Marginal cost should decrease with scale
- Never scale a broken unit

## 3. The 4-Step Business Engine

1. **Get the Ad** - Organic + CTA. Hook is 80% of effectiveness
2. **Get the Lead** - Grand slam offer: stack bonuses, guarantees, risk reversal
3. **Obtainment** - Pre-educate before sales call. 35% close rate = appropriately priced
4. **Retention** - If you focus on one thing, make it retention

## 4. Service Business Scaling

### The Trap
- Trading time for money. Keyman risk: if you are the product, you have a job

### 4 Steps to Unf*** Your Business
1. Systemize delivery (SOPs)
2. Hire for redundancy
3. Productize the offer (fixed scope, price, timeline)
4. Raise prices

### Keyman Risk Fix
- Codify innovation through R&D. Business should run without you for 90 days

## 5. The $100M Offer Design Framework

### Grand Slam Stack
1. Core product/service
2. Bonuses (10x perceived value)
3. Risk reversal (guarantee)
4. Scarcity/urgency

### Price Anchoring
- Perceived value must be 10x price. "They'd be stupid not to" test

### Decommoditization
- If price-compared, you're a commodity. Differentiate on speed, certainty, convenience, community, exclusivity, outcomes

## 6. 2026 Playbook

- AI is a force multiplier, not a shortcut
- What works: organic + retargeting ads, high-ticket offers, community retention, AI-assisted marketing, alternative education

## 7. The 4 Levers (in order)

1. Fix retention (get to month 6, then 2%/mo churn)
2. Raise prices (use close rate data)
3. Fix the offer (grand slam stack)
4. Scale distribution (organic to paid ads)
